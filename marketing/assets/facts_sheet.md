## Caveats to state in posts
- Macro-F1 varies from 0.10 to 0.55 between groups of unseen wells; quote mean ± std, never a single fold.
- The macro-F1 gain over v0.1 (+0.02) is within fold noise; the real gains are fewer false alarms and more events caught.
- Hydrate in production line is partly an instrumentation fingerprint: F1 0.89 → 0.77 without one sensor (T-MON-CKP, on 9 of 40 wells), → 0.50 with common sensors only.
- Severe slugging: 31 of 32 real instances come from one well; an alarm fires, but the event type is never identified (F1 0) and it is effectively tested on one fold.
- Rare events (BSW increase, PCK restriction) have too few real instances for meaningful rates.
- Decision support, not a safety system.
