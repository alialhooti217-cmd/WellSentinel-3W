## Caveats

- Hydrate in production line: part of the skill is an instrumentation fingerprint. Without T-MON-CKP (present on 9 of 40 wells) F1 falls 0.89 → 0.77; with only common sensors → 0.50 (E08).
- Severe slugging: 31 of 32 real instances come from one well (WELL-00014); it is effectively tested on one fold and is not learned (F1 0). Its strict detection rate only means that some alarm fires during the event; the event type is never identified, and there is no early warning (slugging recordings start inside the event).
- Classes with few real instances (BSW increase, PCK restriction) give anecdotal rates.
- Operator signals (valve states, gas-lift rate, choke openings) carry 9 % of sensor importance.
